**Trend window:** September 19–21, 2026  
**Signal:** AI is moving from chat assistants to systems that plan, execute, and update work.

## What is trending?

Asana is preparing an **Agentic Work Management** platform that combines AI Studio and AI Teammates with its project-management suite. The company says AI products contributed 25% of net new ARR in its latest quarter, showing that office AI is becoming a business model—not only a convenience feature.

Anthropic also merged Claude Chat and Cowork into one interface. The new experience is designed to handle quick questions as well as multi-step work, while Claude Docs and Claude Slides let users create collaborative documents and presentations from natural-language instructions.

Meeting software is becoming more automatic too. Google Meet is changing its automatic note-taking behavior on September 21: for some plans, it activates by default for meetings with three or more people.

## Why it matters

The office assistant is evolving into a **workflow teammate**. Instead of asking, “Summarize this,” users increasingly ask, “Review these notes, create tasks, draft the update, and prepare slides.”

## Try this interactive test

> **Prompt:** “Turn this meeting transcript into decisions, owners, deadlines, risks, and a five-line status update.”

Then check:

- Did the AI distinguish decisions from discussion?
- Are owners and dates verified?
- Does every task have a human reviewer?

## Watch-outs

Automation can silently create incorrect tasks, expose confidential meeting content, or make assumptions about deadlines. Keep approval gates for external messages, HR material, finance, and customer commitments.

## Bottom line

The hype is shifting from “AI writes faster” to “AI coordinates work.” The winning office tools will be those that connect documents, meetings, calendars, and project systems without removing human approval.
